How To Play

  1. Reach the glowing gem. Move with the Arrow Keys or WASD, one step per press. On a phone, swipe the room.
  2. Avoid the traps. Touch one and you restart the room. No lives, so just try again.
  3. Traps have a rhythm. Watch one for a moment, then move on the safe beat. Don't stand still.
  4. Start and gem are always safe.

💡 A new trap gets a short card the first time you meet it, so you never have to memorize this page.

Good to know

  • ⭐ Stars: fewest moves = 3 stars. Any finish = 1.
  • 🔓 Unlocking: beat 8 levels of a world to open the next. Beat all three for the bonus castle.
  • 🔑 Levers: the gem is barred until every lever is on.
  • ⏱ Timed rooms: beat the clock or the room resets.
  • 🛠 Creative Mode: build your own room and share it with a link.

Trap guide

Tap a world to see its traps.

All worlds
  • Spikes hurt on touch. Timed spikes: deadly 2 beats, safe 2.
  • Hidden spikes look like plain floor.
  • One-way tiles only let you in the way the arrow points.
  • Portals warp you to their twin.
Volcanic Depths
  • Ice: you keep sliding until you hit something.
  • Currents shove you one extra tile.
  • Turrets fire every 3 beats. Walk with the shots, not against them.
  • Vents glow, then erupt. Fire imps pace back and forth.
  • Bombs light when stepped on. Get 2 tiles clear.
Frozen Caverns
  • Thin ice breaks once you step off. Cross each tile once.
  • Frostbite: safe to cross, deadly to stand on.
  • Icicles fall 3 beats after you walk under them, then become a solid block.
Overgrown Ruins
  • Vines hold you in place for 2 beats.
  • Mushrooms bounce you 2 tiles, over anything between.
  • Snapjaws bite the 4 tiles around them every 6 beats.
  • Wild boars charge 2 tiles per beat.
Abandoned Castle
  • Crushers: up 3 beats (shaking on the third), slam, then stay down and solid for 2.
  • Pressure plates flip every portcullis. They start shut.
  • The ghost chases you through walls, but can't enter the start or gem.
  • Crossbows, barrels, mirrors and floorboards act like turrets, bombs, portals and thin ice.
