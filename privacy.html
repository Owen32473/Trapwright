Privacy Policy

Trapwright · Last updated September 25, 2026

Trapwright is a free puzzle game made by one independent developer. This page explains what information the game collects, why, and how you can delete it. The short version: you can play without an account and nothing about you is collected. If you make an account, we store your email, username and game progress so you can play on any device, and you can delete all of it at any time.

Playing without an account

The game saves your progress (levels beaten, Daily Puzzle streaks, best scores) in your own browser's storage, on your device. It isn't sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data, or "Reset progress" in the game, removes it.

What we collect if you make an account

  • Your email address, to log you in and to send a password-reset link when you ask for one.
  • Your password, which is never stored as you typed it. Our login provider stores only a scrambled (hashed) version that can't be turned back into your password. We never see it.
  • Your username and profile icon. These are private to you; the game doesn't show them to other players.
  • Your game progress: the same levels, streaks and best scores listed above, so they follow you across devices.
  • Basic security information that the login provider keeps automatically, such as when you created your account, when you last logged in, and the IP address used, to protect accounts from abuse.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • No analytics services.
  • We never sell, rent or share your information with anyone for marketing.
  • We never email you except the password-reset emails you ask for.

Who stores it

Accounts and saves are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which runs the servers for us. Google handles this data on our behalf under its own privacy and security terms. Your data may be stored on servers outside your country.

Your browser also keeps you logged in between visits using its local storage. That stays on your device.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. There's no automatic deletion of inactive accounts.

Deleting your account

Log in, click your icon in the top-right corner, then Delete account. After you confirm with your password, your account, username and cloud save are deleted immediately and permanently. Progress saved in that browser stays on your device, and you can clear it with "Reset progress" or by clearing your browser data.

Children

Anyone can play without an account. Accounts are only for people 13 or older, and signing up asks you to confirm that. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

You can see your username and progress in the game at any time, and delete everything with the Delete account button. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), you may have further rights, such as asking for a copy of your data. Contact us and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
